<h1 align="center">Support Policy</h1>

**Effective date:** September 10, 2026

This Support Policy describes the support available for the Continuo app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Continuo is maintained by Moinuddin Ahmad / moin.shift() ("we" or "us").

## Contact support

Email [**support@iamshift.dev**](mailto:support@iamshift.dev).

Please include:
- a short description of what happened and the result you expected;
- Continuo version, device model, operating system, and (if relevant) whether the source was Photos or Files;
- steps to reproduce and any visible error message; and
- a screenshot of the App's own error message, if helpful. Do not include private screenshots or personal information unless you are comfortable sharing them.

You can also review project documentation and report reproducible bugs through the repository's GitHub Issues, subject to the repository guidelines.

## What support covers

We will make reasonable efforts to help with:

- importing still images from Photos or Files;
- vertical or horizontal stitching, alignment, previews, history, and export;
- Photos permissions, iCloud history availability, and StoreKit purchase errors; and
- identifying regressions or reproducible defects in supported releases.

Support is provided in English unless we agree otherwise. We may ask you to update the App, confirm permissions, or provide non-sensitive diagnostic details.

## Response targets

We aim to acknowledge email within **five business days**. This is a target, not a guarantee. Response times may be longer for weekends, holidays, complex investigations, or issues that depend on Apple services. We do not promise a fix, a particular release date, or support for every device configuration.

## Supported environments

Continuo's supported operating systems and requirements are listed in the repository README and the App Store listing. Simulator behavior may differ from physical devices, especially for Photos permissions, iCloud propagation, and full-resolution downloads. We may stop troubleshooting versions of the App or operating systems that Apple no longer supports.

## Data and confidentiality

Stitching runs on-device; support does not require sending us your source screenshots. If you choose to send an image, log, or purchase receipt, send only what is necessary and redact sensitive content. We handle information you send under the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

We will not ask for your Apple Account password, device passcode, payment-card number, or recovery codes. Never include them in a support email.

## Purchases and refunds

Optional support purchases are one-time consumables processed by Apple. We cannot access or change App Store billing. For a refund, billing dispute, or purchase made with the wrong Apple Account, use Apple's official refund and purchase-support process. Include the App Store transaction details only through Apple's secure channels. We can investigate an App-side failure when you provide the product name, approximate date, and the error shown.

## Safety and limitations

Continuo is an image utility, not a backup, records-management, medical, legal, or emergency service. Keep originals and independent backups of anything important. We are not responsible for loss caused by deleting source items, device failure, insufficient storage, iCloud outages, or actions taken based on an unverified result.

## Abuse and urgent reports

Do not use support to send unlawful, threatening, abusive, or confidential third-party information. Report security vulnerabilities privately to [**support@iamshift.dev**](mailto:support@iamshift.dev) with “Security” in the subject; do not open a public issue containing an exploit or private data. For an immediate threat to safety, contact local emergency services.

## Changes

We may update this policy as our support channels or the App change. The current version will remain available at this URL.
